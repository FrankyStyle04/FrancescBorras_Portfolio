Denshattack!

Arcade Platformer / Unreal Engine 5

2025 - 2026

Denshattack! is an arcade train-flipping platformer published by Fireshine Games.

I worked as QA and Game Design Assistant, balancing progression across the whole game and owning the design documentation.

Task Overview

  • Balanced progression and economy across 67 handcrafted levels.
  • Authored 6 Game Design Documents covering systems and implementation.
  • Validated level flow and difficulty through structured playtesting.
  • Maintained system documentation in Confluence for cross-team work.
View on Steam More projects

My Contributions

My job had to survive the jump from documentation to real implementation, not just read well in a PDF. That meant staying close to both the design intent and the day-to-day production reality of a shipping project.

  • Progression and economy balance Balanced 67 handcrafted levels, tuning the unlock pace in Excel and Google Sheets so difficulty and rewards escalated at a rate that felt earned rather than grindy.
  • Design documentation Authored 6 comprehensive Game Design Documents covering gameplay systems, progression logic and implementation requirements for a multidisciplinary team.
  • Living documentation in Confluence Kept system documentation current so designers, programmers and QA could all work from the same source of truth.
  • Gameplay feature support Helped design and refine features alongside designers and programmers, keeping the player experience aligned with project goals.
  • QA and playtest validation Validated level flow and difficulty balance through structured internal playtesting, translating raw feedback into concrete design changes.

What I learned

Working at the intersection of design and QA meant every balance change I proposed had to be re-tested by hand before it could ship. That discipline shaped how I document changes on every project since.
