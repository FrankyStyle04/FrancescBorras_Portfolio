Game Developer specialized in Game Design

Hi! I'm Franky!

Specialized in gameplay systems and level design. I work in both Unreal Engine and Unity, and I have shipped a title published on Steam, Xbox and PlayStation alongside personal projects taken from concept to a validated build.

What I care about is the work that makes a game feel right: pacing, balancing and the small details that decide whether someone stays ten minutes or ten hours. I document mechanics before building them, and I validate them with real players instead of intuition.

Right now I am based near Barcelona and looking for my first full-time role in the industry.
